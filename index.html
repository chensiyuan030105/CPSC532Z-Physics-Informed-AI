01 · About the course

Where scientific insight meets modern AI.

CPSC 532Z explores methods for incorporating physical laws and scientific priors into machine-learning systems. We will connect theory with hands-on experiments across scientific machine learning, differential equations, and simulation.

Instructor / course contact: Peter Yichen Chen
